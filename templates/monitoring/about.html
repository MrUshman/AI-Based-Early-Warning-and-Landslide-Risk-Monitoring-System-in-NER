Multi-Agency Coordination & Workflow Architecture

How the early warning software bridges geotechnical data with India's disaster management command chain.

Institutional Roles in India's Landslide Disaster Response
Agency Real-World Mandate in India How This Early Warning System Supports It
IMD
India Meteorological Department
 Official weather forecasts, heavy rainfall telemetry, and cloudburst bulletins. In V1, rainfall observations are entered by field personnel informed by IMD rain telemetry. In future iterations, automated IMD API ingestion directly supplies live rainfall values.
GSI
Geological Survey of India
 National nodal agency for landslide hazard zonation and slope stability studies. Locations, slope angles, and lithological data follow GSI baseline classifications. The historical incident repository archives ground failure data for post-monsoon geotechnical calibration.
NDMA / SDMA / DDMA
Disaster Management Authorities
 Statutory bodies directing disaster risk reduction, district coordination, and emergency response. Core system user role: District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) officers receive instant alerts, confirm audit-stamped acknowledgement, and document ground actions before resolution.
NDRF / SDRF
National & State Disaster Response Forces
 Specialized paramilitary search, rescue, and community evacuation operations. Critical alerts provide preemptive geographic coordinates, enabling NDRF battalions to preposition swift-water and rubble-clearing search teams hours before catastrophic failure occurs.
BRO / NHIDCL
Border Roads Organisation & NHIDCL
 Construction and maintenance of strategic Himalayan and border national highways. Highway sectors (NH-10, NH-6, NH-29, NH-415) and road blockage status connect directly to heavy plant (excavators, bulldozers) mobilization and traffic closure decisions.
The End-to-End Early Warning Alert Lifecycle
Step 1: DETECT
Observation Input

Field officers enter rainfall, soil saturation, and ground vibration measurements.

Step 2: RAISE
Explainable Scoring

Score ≥ 50/100 triggers an instant automated alert with plain-language action guidance.

Step 3: ACKNOWLEDGE
DDMA Confirmation

District officer confirms receipt with an audit timestamp and initial response directives.

Step 4: ACT
Ground Interventions

BRO initiates traffic control, police deploy barricades, and SDRF is placed on standby.

Step 5: CLOSE
Resolution & Archive

Once risk subsides or debris is cleared, officer closes the alert; full history is preserved.

Academic Viva & Evaluation Readiness

Why Explainable Scoring instead of opaque Deep Learning? In public safety and disaster administration, civil authorities cannot justify shutting down a national lifeline highway or evacuating a settlement on a black-box model. Every score in this system can be verified with pen and paper by a district collector or viva examiner.

Version 1 Honesty: Uses seeded and manual telemetry with an explainable 4-factor scoring engine. Automated IoT hardware feeds, IMD webhooks, and SMS/WhatsApp dissemination are documented future scope upgrades.
