NER Landslide EWS

An explainable artificial intelligence early warning and slope risk monitoring system covering 12 vulnerable highway corridors and urban slopes across the 8 North Eastern States of India.

Monsoon Focus: Intense precipitation season (June–September) peak vigilance protocol active.
Monitored States
  • Arunachal Pradesh
  • Assam (Dima Hasao/Ghy)
  • Manipur (Ukhrul)
  • Meghalaya (NH-6/Dawki)
  • Mizoram (Hunthar Veng)
  • Nagaland (Kohima NH-29)
  • Sikkim (NH-10/Gangtok)
  • Tripura (Agartala)
Agency Workflow Roles
  • IMD: Rainfall telemetry & heavy precipitation forecasts
  • GSI: Nodal geological hazard mapping & slope inventory
  • NDMA / SDMA: National & State disaster policy coordination
  • DDMA: District response authority & alert actions
  • NDRF / SDRF: Immediate search, rescue & evacuation
  • BRO / NHIDCL: Highway clearance & arterial traffic restoration
Emergency Reference
Emergency Relief Hotline Dial 112
Toll-free 24x7 all-India emergency response support service.

Explainable AI formula: Rainfall (40) + Soil Moisture (25) + Slope (20) + Vibration (15)
