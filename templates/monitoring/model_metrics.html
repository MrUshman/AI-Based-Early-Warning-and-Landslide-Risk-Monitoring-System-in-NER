Viva & Presentation Reference
  • Why Random Forest? Landslide events exhibit non-linear thresholds: a 40° slope is stable when dry, but fails catastrophically when soil saturation exceeds 80%. Ensemble decision trees capture these compound multi-feature interactions without overfitting.
  • Synthetic Dataset Grounding: Modeled after Geological Survey of India (GSI) susceptibility guidelines and IMD rainfall intensity-duration thresholds for the North-East Himalayas (Caine, 1980 empirical formulation).
  • Inference Pipeline: The trained model is serialized via joblib and loaded directly in monitoring/risk_engine.py, enabling sub-millisecond evaluation per incoming sensor packet.
